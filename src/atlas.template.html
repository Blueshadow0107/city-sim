    Heat is the Stadestér 1.0 population raster (rebuilt from HYDE 3.x, Reba et al., Chandler, Modelski and others), snapshots at 1000-year steps before 0 CE and 100-year steps to 1700, then every 10 years. Years between snapshots are interpolated in log space, so 2357 BCE sits between the 3000 and 2000 BCE grids. Dots are observed city populations (whole urban agglomerations), also interpolated; a city is hidden before its first observation and held at its last value after the table ends (about 2000). The dashed line is the satellite-era (GHSL) urban area, which counts differently from the older series.

    The time axis is stretched: 2357 BCE to 0 CE, 0 to 1700, and 1700 to 2025 get 30%, 30% and 40% of the width. Map is equirectangular, so east-west distances look wider than they are away from the equator. Data: Stadestér 1.0 (Zenodo, MIT).
